# Density

How many parts, particles or copies.

**Fixed when** the prompt gives a count ("five fire dragons", "a thousand swords") or a busyness ("a few sparks",
"a dense storm").

**Ask:** "How many?" 2 to 3 counts, each saying what it does to the read at 1x: fewer reads cleaner, more reads
grander but turns to noise past what the cell can hold.

**Examples:**
- Few (1 to 3 parts): the cleanest read at 1x. In a bullet hell like Enter the Gungeon each bullet must stay
  distinct.
- Medium (4 to 10): reads as a group, a volley.
- Many (dozens to hundreds): grand, like the swarms in Vampire Survivors, but it turns to noise past what the cell
  holds. Dead Cells lets players cap particles for that reason.
Debris for a light hit: 3 to 8 pixels; a big burst only for kills or breakables.

**Default:** the fewest that read as the subject at the chosen size.
