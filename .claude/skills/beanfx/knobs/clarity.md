# Clarity

How much of the cell stays empty and dark, and how solid the colour pixels are. Glow sets how bright the light is;
clarity sets where it is allowed to spread. The light rules and the measured targets per level are in SKILL.md 2a.

**Fixed when** the prompt names it ("crisp", "clean", "sparkling", "misty", "hazy", "foggy", "dreamy bloom") or an
image shows it. "Sparkling" or "shimmering" is clear.

**Ask:** "How clear or hazy?" Options:
- "Clear (Recommended): solid pixels on a dark ground, light only on what glows, sparks that flash and die"
- "Balanced: clear shapes, light mist and a small pool round the peak"
- "Hazy: soft translucent volumes and a wide pool, for fog, dreams and auras; reads muddier at 1x"

**Examples:**
- Clear: Dead Cells keeps anything that can hurt the player bright and high-contrast against the background. Pick
  for fast play.
- Balanced: a little mist round the peak; magic and heat.
- Hazy: Dead Cells uses drifting particles and foreground clouds for depth, as set dressing and never for threats.
  On a busy ground a hazy effect needs a dark pad or an outline in the engine.

**Default:** clear.
