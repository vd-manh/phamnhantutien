# Carrier shape

What the moving part is and its silhouette at the peak: bolt, fan, beam, ground crawl, geyser, falling column,
claw, burst, ring, a creature.

**Fixed when** the prompt or an image describes the silhouette. A subject name alone ("fireball", "dragon") does
not fix it, and a word with two readings ("slash") is open.

**Ask** after the web research, so the options come from how the subject is known: "Which shape at the peak?" 2 to
4 silhouettes, each with an ASCII `preview` of the peak frame at about 16 x 8 characters and a line on what it
reads as at 1x (a fireball: round head with broken puffs, a comet teardrop, a spinning ring of flame). Recommend
the one that reads clearest at the chosen size. A reference game or art the owner names gives principles, never a
copy of its effect.

**Examples** (pair each silhouette with one the owner may know):
- Round burst: the fastest read, hides what is behind it. The Dead Cells grenade blast.
- Ring or shockwave: shows the radius and keeps the middle open so the player stays visible. The Enter the Gungeon
  Blank clears bullets in a ring round the player.
- Streak or trail: says speed and direction. A dash or a slash; in pixel art one smear frame (the shape stretched
  along its path for a single frame) does the job, a second only for a charged attack.
- Comet or teardrop: the tail says where it flies. Noita's spark bolt, a fast sparkling shot.
- Solid disc or chunk: reads as an object, not a gas. Noita's giga disc that digs through terrain.
Check the pick as a silhouette: fill the peak frame with one flat colour; if it does not read that way at 1x,
shading will not save it.

**Default:** the carrier the research and the element point to; 3 beats with a 1 to 2 frame peak.
